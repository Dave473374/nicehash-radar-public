# Phone alert latency criterion

This is an infrastructure-only check. It does not change CURRENT 2.9.0, BATCH,
BUY thresholds, market-edge research, order behavior, or NiceHash access.

The purpose is to answer one narrow question: has the existing hourly ChatGPT
phone check become slow enough to miss materially useful short BUY windows?

Only a strict subset can contribute evidence:
- PRIMARY packages only: S plus USDT 5 and 20.
- CURRENT must be BUY NOW or STRONG BUY.
- feed EV must be at least 95%.
- profitability must be complete.
- MATH must be PASS or NOT_APPLICABLE.
- the historical snapshot must have been captured within 7 minutes of its feed
  timestamp.

GOOD signals, MATH WARNING, CRITICAL and UNKNOWN do not drive the infrastructure
upgrade. They may still have their separate alert behavior, but they cannot make
this report recommend faster push delivery.

A window is resolved only when a later valid snapshot shows that the strict
condition is no longer true. Gaps above 12 minutes censor the window instead of
guessing its duration.

The report recommends a 5-minute phone path only after at least 3 resolved strict
windows in the current relay/decision-engine regime and either:
1. at least 2 windows are at most 45 minutes long and at least half of all
   resolved windows are that short; or
2. at least one STRONG BUY window is at most 30 minutes long.

The 3-window minimum is intentionally conservative. A short window shows that an
hourly poll can miss an opportunity; it does not prove a particular historical
phone run actually missed it.

Output: research/alert-latency-report.json

The report is generated by the existing Public Market Edge Research workflow.
There is no new collector or extra NiceHash request. The report can only recommend
delivery infrastructure; it cannot raise a BUY signal and cannot buy or cancel
anything.
